📂 Categories & Setup
How do I add a new category?

Tap "Add Category" on the Home screen, or go to Settings → Categories.

  • Free plan: 1 category
  • Premium plan: Unlimited categories
How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language → Select your preferred language.

SHUGYO supports 11 languages.

🤖 AI Coach
How does the AI Coach work?

The AI Coach analyzes your training records and goals to provide personalized advice.

  • Free plan: 1 AI session per month (trial)
  • Premium plan: Unlimited (up to 200 sessions/month)
Why does the AI Coach not respond to my message?

The AI Coach focuses on self-improvement topics only. For unrelated topics, the AI may decline to respond.

Please also ensure you have a stable internet connection.

Free: 1 category, 1 AI session/month, Simple theme only

How do I cancel Premium?
  1. Open iPhone Settings
  2. Tap your Apple ID at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Select "SHUGYO"
  5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"

Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.

📋 Templates
How do I share my template?
  1. Go to Settings → Templates
  2. Select your category
  3. Generate share code

Send the SHUGYO-XXXXX code to anyone with SHUGYO installed.

How do I import a template?
  1. Go to Settings → Templates
  2. Tap Import
  3. Paste the SHUGYO-XXXXX code
  4. Tap Import
💾 Data & Privacy
My data disappeared after reinstalling

Data is stored locally on your device.

We recommend using the Export JSON feature regularly as a backup.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your training data is stored locally on your device only.

AI coaching messages are sent to Anthropic API but are not retained.
