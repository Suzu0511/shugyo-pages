Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 2026
1

Data We Collect

SHUGYO is designed with privacy as a priority.

  • Your training records (categories, exercises, values, notes, dates), language, and theme settings are stored locally on your device only.
  • SHUGYO does not require account registration and does not collect your name, email address, or location.
2

AI Features & Data Sent to a Third-Party AI Service

SHUGYO offers optional AI features: the AI Coach and the AI Goal Assistant.

What data is sent: When you use these features, the text messages you type and the relevant training records needed to generate a response are transmitted.

Who it is sent to: This data is sent to Anthropic, Inc. (the Claude API), the third-party AI service provider that generates the coaching responses and goal suggestions.

How it is collected and used: The data is transmitted only when you actively use an AI feature, and only after you have given your consent within the app. It is used solely to generate the AI response shown to you. SHUGYO does not store this data on its own servers.

We do not send any audio, voice data, or personally identifying information (such as your name, email, or location) to the AI service. AI features are entirely optional — you can use SHUGYO as a tracker without using them.

Anthropic processes this data in accordance with its own privacy policy, which provides protections comparable to those described in this policy. Data submitted through the Anthropic API is not used to train Anthropic's models.

Anthropic Privacy Policy →

3

Your Consent

Before any data is sent to the AI service, SHUGYO asks for your explicit consent within the app the first time you use an AI feature.

  • If you do not consent, no data is sent and the AI features are not used. All other features (recording, statistics, history, manual goals) remain fully available.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time from the Settings screen. After withdrawing, AI features will request your consent again before sending any data.
4

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email, or location.
  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not use tracking technologies or advertising IDs.
5

Third Party Services

SHUGYO uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic, Inc. (Claude API) — used to generate AI Coach responses and AI Goal suggestions. The text you enter and relevant training records are transmitted to Anthropic to produce a response. Anthropic provides data protection comparable to that described in this policy.
    Anthropic Privacy Policy →
  • Apple App Store — for subscription management and payment processing.

We share data with these third parties only to provide the features described above, and we confirm that these providers offer comparable data protection.

6

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to SHUGYO Premium, subscription data is managed by Apple.

We do not have access to your payment information.
7

Data Security

All personal data is stored locally on your device.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

8

Your Rights

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app
  • Using the data reset option in the Settings screen

As all data is stored locally, no further request to us is necessary.

9

Children's Privacy

SHUGYO is intended for users aged 4 and above.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

10

International Users

SHUGYO complies with major privacy regulations:

  • GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • Personal Information Protection Act of Japan
11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Continued use of SHUGYO after changes means you accept the updated policy.
