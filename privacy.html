Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 2026
1

Data We Collect

SHUGYO is designed with privacy as a priority.

  • Training records, language, and theme settings are stored locally on your device only.
  • When you use AI coaching features, your input is sent to Anthropic API to generate responses. This data is not retained by SHUGYO.
2

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email, or location.
  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not use tracking technologies or advertising IDs.
3

Third Party Services

SHUGYO uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API for AI coaching features.
    Anthropic Privacy Policy →
  • Apple App Store for subscription management and payment processing.
4

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to SHUGYO Premium, subscription data is managed by Apple.

We do not have access to your payment information.
5

Data Security

All personal data is stored locally on your device.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6

Your Rights

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app
  • Using the data reset option in the Settings screen

As all data is stored locally, no further request to us is necessary.

7

Children's Privacy

SHUGYO is intended for users aged 4 and above.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

8

International Users

SHUGYO complies with major privacy regulations:

  • GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • Personal Information Protection Act of Japan
9

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Continued use of SHUGYO after changes means you accept the updated policy.
